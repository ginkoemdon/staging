Plain-Language Government AI

Dense government text, rewritten so anyone can read it — with sources kept, and unsure phrases flagged instead of guessed.

How to use this product

  1. Read the sample — a dense government text about an accessibility benefit is already loaded. Edit it if you like.
  2. Tap “Rewrite in plain language” — the plain version appears beside the original, with a reading grade before and after.
  3. Check the flags, then copy — highlighted phrases are ones the AI is unsure about. Check them in the source, then copy the plain version.

What happens when you do it: you get a plain-language draft, every sentence linked to its source, and the AI's uncertain phrases highlighted with a note — it never guesses. Sample data only. No text is sent anywhere: this demo runs entirely in your browser on a pre-written sample and does not call any AI service. If you get stuck, use “Reset to sample” to start again, or go back to Ginko's Prototype Playground.

You can apply for the accessibility benefit if you meet the rules. Send in your form, plus documents that show how your disability affects your daily life. You have 90 days from the date we tell you your decision.Source: Accessibility Benefit Regulations, s. 4(2) — eligibility, and the 90-day deadline.

If you do not send the documents in time, your payments may stop.AI is unsure — check source: the original says “suspension or termination.” That could mean a pause or a full stop. This rewrite does not guess which one — confirm it in the source before publishing.Source: Accessibility Benefit Regulations — late documents, suspension / termination, and reconsideration.

You can ask us to look at your case again if you send the documents later.Source: Accessibility Benefit Regulations — reconsideration on submission of additional evidence.

We decide based on the information we have when we look at your file. If you are approved, back pay will only cover up to 12 months before we got your full application.AI is unsure — check source: confirm whether the 12 months are counted from when we received your full application, or from when we approved it.Source: Accessibility Benefit Regulations — decisions on the information on file, and the 12-month retroactive limit.

If you have questions, contact the Benefit Administration Division during regular business hours.Source: Accessibility Benefit Regulations — contact information.

Readability is a rough Flesch–Kincaid grade level, from word and sentence counts in your browser. Lower is easier to read. It is a guide, not a verdict — plain language is also about short sentences, familiar words, and clear next steps.
