Co-Design Synthesis

Turn a pile of workshop notes into clear themes, so everyone can see what was actually said — and what was missing.

How to use this product

  1. Read the 6 sample notes — workshop notes about reimagining a public space in Tiohtià:ke / Montréal.
  2. Press “Synthesise themes” — the prototype groups the 6 notes into 3 themes. Use the filter buttons to look at one theme at a time.
  3. Check each theme — every theme shows the exact quote from its source note, so you can trace it back. The box at the end lists what the notes did not cover.

Sample data only. All names and quotes on this page are invented for this prototype, not real people. No theme is made up to fill a gap — if a theme has no supporting quote, it is not shown. Nothing you do here is sent anywhere.

What was NOT found — needs more discussion

With only 6 sample notes, these themes are provisional. The synthesis did not find:

  • Winter use: no note talks about how the space should work in winter, or who clears it.
  • Upkeep and cost: no note mentions maintenance, budget, or who looks after the space over time.
  • Agreement on the monument: the notes disagree on what should happen to the existing monument — that needs a dedicated discussion, not a vote from these 6 notes.
  • Youth voices: no sample note is from a young person. Before deciding, gather notes from youth and elders who were not in this sample.

No theme was made up to fill these gaps. If a theme has no supporting quote, it is not shown.
