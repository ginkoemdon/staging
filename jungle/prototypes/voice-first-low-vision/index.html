Voice-First AI for Low Vision

Speak, listen, then confirm. Nothing happens until you say so.

How to use this product

  1. Choose a sample — pick a product label or an appointment below.
  2. Press “Read it aloud” — the AI reads it in large text, slowly. Then answer by voice, or type instead.
  3. Press Confirm — only the Confirm button acts. A voice “yes” alone never books, sends, or saves anything.

When you confirm, the demo shows a “Confirmed” message and nothing else changes — nothing is booked, sent, or saved, and nothing leaves your browser.

Sample data only. No backend, no external AI calls. It runs entirely in your browser, using the Web Speech API when your browser supports it.

How this prototype is designed

  • Voice first, not voice only: every spoken step has a large button and a typed fallback.
  • Confirm before acting: the AI reads, you answer, then you press Confirm. A voice “yes” alone never books, sends, or saves anything.
  • Slow, clear speech: the readout is spoken at a slower rate, and shown in very large text at the same time.
  • Uncertain? It says so: if the browser can’t hear you, or the answer isn’t clear, it asks again instead of guessing.
