Pixel Jungle — an experiment

A tropical jungle drawn in code, one pixel at a time, following the way Ben Emdon built his pixel meadow. A river streams through the middle, sunlight beams through the canopy, and butterflies fly by day while fireflies glow after dark.

Experiment only. This page is not linked from the landing page or the All prototypes page. It lives here on preview so the pixel scene is kept, not lost, after the hero was replaced with a smooth illustration in Ginko's own style. Day or night follows your device's light or dark setting.

180 × 72 cells, built once from a fixed seed, re-lit day and night over the same shapes. Nothing moves smoothly — clouds step, the river shimmers one shade at a time.
