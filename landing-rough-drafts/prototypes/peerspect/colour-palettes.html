PEERSPECT colour palettes

Four palettes, one brand core: a warm, human, steady guide, walking beside you, not pulling you. Informed autonomy first. Easy, simple, intuitive and clear, so choosing feels calm, not overwhelming.

Brand core, agreed Nov 23, 2026: warm and human, steady guide, informed autonomy and agency, reduce overwhelm and decision fatigue, build trust and confidence, centre customisable preferences.

Rule for every palette below: all text is WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 or better). Bright colours are accents only, never small text.

Body copy is 16px or larger with line height 1.6 or more, and all text pairs pass WCAG 2.2 AA in Light and Dark. Try each pairing in Light and Dark with its toggle. Spacing: more air around elements

Spacing is the air between things. When it is too tight, everything feels condensed and hard to scan. When it is generous, each element gets room to breathe and the page feels calm. Spacing here follows Apple iOS Human Interface Guidelines: an 8pt base grid, 16pt side margins, 20pt between sections, generous card padding, and 44pt minimum touch targets. iOS points map to CSS pixels on the web, so 16pt reads as 16px here. Everything below uses the locked Warmer Teal palette: warm white #fdfbf7 in Light, soft teal night #173a33 and #214f45 in Dark. All text still passes WCAG 2.2 AA in Light and Dark.

The spacing scale

One scale, used everywhere, so spacing stays consistent instead of guessed each time. The base unit is 4px, and every step is a multiple of it. The iOS values are marked.

  • 4pxTiny gaps: inside chips, between an icon and its text.
  • 8pxiOS base grid unit. Small gaps: between chips, between a label and the text under it.
  • 12pxBetween related items: card inner spacing, list rows.
  • 16pxiOS side margins. Base unit: between paragraphs, side padding on buttons.
  • 20pxiOS section spacing, and generous card padding. Between groups of choices, between the onboarding block and the place card.
  • 24pxBetween cards, airy gaps between sections.
  • 32pxBetween major sections inside one screen, very generous card padding.
  • 44pxiOS minimum touch target. Buttons and tappable rows are at least 44pt tall, so they are easy to tap.
  • 48pxBetween major sections, top and bottom padding of a screen.
  • 64pxBig breathing room: between screens, around the hero moment.

iOS spacing values used here: 8pt base grid, 16pt side margins, 20pt section spacing, 20pt card padding, 44pt minimum touch targets, and 8pt gaps between chips. Clear grouping with air between sections, not condensed.

Three spacing options, same sample

The same onboarding card and place card, at three densities. Compact is tight and condensed, below the iOS guidance. Comfortable and Airy are the generous ones, and both are recommended. Airy is visibly roomier: more padding, more gap between sections, chips and buttons.

Warm Adventure, revised: same feeling, new colours

Ginko still enjoys the Warm Adventure / Everyday Adventure feeling — warm, finding a place is a small everyday adventure, the thread is a path, clean with breathing room, stimulating with one vivid pop. The colours underneath it, though, were too close to Ginko's personal brand, so they have been swapped out. The feeling stays; the hues change.

What was swapped out, and why: deep teal (the anchor), forest (the deep surface), sun (the path), tangerine-dark (the warm text accent), and cream (the background) — all too close to the personal-brand family. What replaced them: a deep berry-plum anchor instead of teal/forest, a new warm clay-cream background instead of cream, a plum-night dark background, a peach-clay surface, and a vivid citrus pop for the path instead of sun — with a citrus-dark text accent in light mode and a citrus-light accent in dark mode.
