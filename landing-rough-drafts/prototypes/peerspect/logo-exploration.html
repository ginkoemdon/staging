How to choose

A is the warmest, B is the clearest, C is the most adventurous. All three keep deep teal as the anchor, the thread/path visible, and text at AA contrast.

Mixing is welcome: e.g. Brand B's clarity with Brand A's warm peach surfaces, or Brand C's thread with Brand A's Shared Eye.

Logo 4 Compass Eye is not paired above, but it swaps cleanly into Brand C — same compass / wayfinding feeling, same Baloo 2 wordmark.

Which feels most like PEERSPECT? Tell me a number — 1, 2, 3 or 4 for the logo, and A, B or C for the whole brand — or mix them: e.g. the Map + Thread mark with the Atkinson Hyperlegible wordmark, in Brand B.

All marks are code-only SVG, no images. Colours are the PEERSPECT teal #0b5e54 on off-white #f8f7f4, AA contrast for text. Fonts load from Google Fonts, with system fallbacks if offline.
