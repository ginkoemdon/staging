Tropical jungle

A jungle drawn in code, not a picture file. A river streams through the middle, sunlight beams through the canopy, and after dark the moon comes out and fireflies blink in the undergrowth. Nothing slides or fades: clouds step, the breeze and the river shimmer step one shade at a time, and butterflies step one cell at a time.

Day or night follows the light or dark setting. Try it:

How it is built: one scene, built once as a grid of coloured cells from a fixed seed, so it looks the same on every load. Day and night are the same grid lit twice. If you use reduced motion, you get the finished scene in one still picture, with no breeze, no shimmer, and no blinking fireflies.
